Football Top 10 Quiz

Club Ten is a free daily football top-10 quiz, the tenaball experience built around your Premier League club and refreshed every single day. If you love football tenaball and tenable quizzes, Club Ten is the club-specific version: a new top-10 puzzle for your club every day. Name ten players, managers or records in a category before your three lives run out.

How it works

Every day at midnight London time a new top-10 puzzle drops for each club. Type a name into the box — correct answers fill a green slot, wrong guesses cost a life. Lose all three lives and the remaining answers are revealed. Find all ten to win.

Past puzzles have included Premier League top scorers, most-capped players, Champions League appearance makers, players with the most yellow cards, and the goalkeepers with the most clean sheets — categories that reward real football knowledge.

Your club. Every day.

Unlike a generic football quiz, every Club Ten puzzle is built specifically for one club. Arsenal fans get Arsenal questions. Spurs fans get Spurs questions. Your streak and scores are saved in your browser — no account, no sign-up, nothing to install.
