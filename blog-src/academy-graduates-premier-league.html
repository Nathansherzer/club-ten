Most Premier League Appearances by Academy Graduates at Every Big Six Club

By Club Ten · 28 August 2026 · Updated through the end of the 2025/26 season

In an era of nine-figure transfer fees, academy graduates who go on to make hundreds of Premier League appearances for the club that developed them are increasingly rare.

From Ryan Giggs and John Terry to Bukayo Saka and Phil Foden, these are the 10 academy graduates with the most Premier League appearances for each of the Premier League's Big Six clubs.

This list overlaps with Club Ten's 18 August daily puzzles across all six clubs. Spoiler warning if you haven't played those yet.

Arsenal academy graduates with the most Premier League appearances

Ray Parlour leads Arsenal's homegrown list, a sign of how consistently the club's academy has produced first-team players across different generations. Tony Adams, a one-club Arsenal legend who captained the Gunners to two Premier League titles, sits second.

Bukayo Saka is already third and still in his mid-twenties, giving him a realistic chance of eventually challenging the two players above him. Cesc Fàbregas and Ashley Cole, meanwhile, represent an outstanding period for Arsenal's youth system in the early 2000s.

Chelsea academy graduates with the most Premier League appearances

John Terry stands comfortably at the top of Chelsea's list, having made 492 Premier League appearances for the club. Captain, five-time Premier League champion and one of Chelsea's greatest players, Terry is the defining academy graduate of the club's modern history.

Behind him are graduates from very different eras. Eddie Newton and Frank Sinclair emerged before Chelsea became one of English football's biggest spenders, while Reece James, Mason Mount, Trevoh Chalobah and Conor Gallagher represent the modern Cobham production line.

Liverpool academy graduates with the most Premier League appearances

Manchester City academy graduates with the most Premier League appearances

Phil Foden leads Manchester City's homegrown list and is perhaps the clearest example of what the club's modern academy can produce. After progressing through City's youth system, he became a central part of Pep Guardiola's dominant Premier League teams.

Micah Richards, Shaun Wright-Phillips, Stephen Ireland and Nedum Onuoha represent an earlier generation, before City's transformation into the dominant force of the modern Premier League era. Rico Lewis represents the newest generation coming through the club's academy.

Manchester United academy graduates with the most Premier League appearances

Manchester United's Class of '92 gives the club arguably the most famous academy story of the Premier League era.

Ryan Giggs, Paul Scholes, Gary Neville, Nicky Butt, David Beckham and Phil Neville all feature among United's leading academy graduates. Together, they formed the core of teams that dominated English football under Sir Alex Ferguson.

Marcus Rashford later carried that tradition into the modern era, climbing to fourth on United's list of academy graduates by Premier League appearances.

One generation, six spots

No Big Six club can quite match what Manchester United's academy produced during one extraordinary generation.

Six of United's top seven academy graduates by Premier League appearances — Giggs, Scholes, Gary Neville, Butt, Beckham and Phil Neville — emerged within a few years of each other during the early 1990s and became synonymous with the Class of '92.

The other clubs' lists are generally spread across several distinct periods. Arsenal's leading graduates, for example, range from Tony Adams and Ray Parlour to Cesc Fàbregas, Ashley Cole and Bukayo Saka. Liverpool's list stretches from Fowler and McManaman through Gerrard and Carragher to Alexander-Arnold and Jones.

United's list is different. A remarkable proportion of its Premier League academy legacy came from one extraordinary period.

The current generation is already climbing the rankings

Perhaps the most interesting pattern across the six clubs is how highly several modern academy graduates already rank.

Phil Foden is first at Manchester City. Bukayo Saka is third at Arsenal. Trent Alexander-Arnold reached fourth at Liverpool before leaving for Real Madrid, while Marcus Rashford reached fourth at Manchester United.

That means four of the Big Six have had a player from the modern academy era reach their club's top four for Premier League appearances by academy graduates. And some of these rankings could still change considerably.

Saka and Foden, in particular, have the opportunity to put significant distance between themselves and many of the players below them if they remain at their clubs for the prime years of their careers.

Which Big Six club has the strongest academy record?

Manchester United has the strongest historical case based on longevity at the very top of its list. The Class of '92 alone produced six players who rank among the club's leading academy graduates of the Premier League era.

Arsenal and Liverpool stand out for something slightly different: longevity across generations. Both clubs have produced significant first-team players during multiple periods of the Premier League era.

Manchester City's list tells another story. Its academy has become substantially more prominent during the modern era, with Foden and Lewis demonstrating the increasing importance of City's youth system alongside its enormous spending power.

Chelsea's Cobham academy has also produced a remarkable concentration of Premier League-level talent in recent years, even though many graduates have ultimately continued their careers elsewhere.

Methodology

For this list, an academy graduate is defined as a player who joined the club's youth or academy system before making his senior first-team debut for any club, and who subsequently made his senior competitive debut for the Big Six club in question.

Players do not need to have spent their entire youth career at that club. This means players such as Cesc Fàbregas, Héctor Bellerín and Raheem Sterling qualify because they joined their respective academies before making their senior debuts.

Loan spells do not disqualify a player.

Players are ranked by total Premier League appearances for the club, including both starts and substitute appearances. Players level on appearances are shown as tied.

Data runs through the end of the 2025/26 Premier League season. Figures are cross-checked against official club and Premier League records before publication.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Manchester United's leading academy graduate by Premier League appearances?

Ryan Giggs leads Manchester United's academy graduates with 632 Premier League appearances for the club.

Does Cesc Fàbregas count as an Arsenal academy graduate?

Under the methodology used for this list, yes. Fàbregas joined Arsenal from Barcelona's youth system at 16 and made his senior debut for Arsenal before playing first-team football elsewhere.

Does Raheem Sterling count as a Liverpool academy graduate?

Yes under this methodology. Sterling joined Liverpool's academy from Queens Park Rangers as a teenager and made his senior debut for Liverpool before making a first-team appearance for another club.

Which Big Six club produced the Class of '92?

Manchester United. The famous generation included Ryan Giggs, Paul Scholes, David Beckham, Gary Neville, Nicky Butt and Phil Neville, all of whom went on to make significant numbers of Premier League appearances for United.
