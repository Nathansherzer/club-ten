The Last 10 Permanent Captains at Every Big Six Club

By Club Ten · Published 30 August 2026 · Primary permanent captains only, not vice-captains or one-off stand-ins

Every club handles the captaincy differently. Some hand it to one player for over a decade; others rotate it every season or two. Here is the full succession of permanent captains at each of the Big Six, most recent first.

This list overlaps with Club Ten's 17 August daily puzzles across all six clubs — spoiler warning if you haven't played those yet.

Arsenal's last 10 permanent captains

Arsenal's captaincy has moved fastest in the post-Wenger years — six different captains between 2016 and 2022 alone, compared with just two across the entire 1988–2005 period under Tony Adams and Patrick Vieira. Martin Ødegaard has now held the armband since 2022, longer than any of his four immediate predecessors combined.

Chelsea's last 10 permanent captains

John Terry's 13 years as captain (2004–2017) is the longest single reign anywhere in this list — more than the last five Chelsea captains combined. Before Terry, the armband changed hands roughly every two seasons through the late 1980s and 1990s, until Dennis Wise settled it down for eight years from 1993.

Liverpool's last 10 permanent captains

Steven Gerrard's 12 years (2003–2015) anchors the middle of this list, followed by Jordan Henderson's eight-year reign. The early 1990s were far choppier by comparison: the armband changed hands twice in three seasons — from Ronnie Whelan to Mark Wright to Ian Rush — before Rush settled it down for three years from 1993.

Manchester City's last 10 permanent captains

City's captaincy tells the story of the club's transformation. Sylvain Distin and Richard Dunne captained a mid-table side in the years before the 2008 takeover; Vincent Kompany then held the armband for eight years through the club's rise to dominance, longer than any City captain before or since. The five captains since Kompany's departure have each served one to two years apiece.

Tottenham's last 10 permanent captains

Gary Mabbutt's 11 years (1987–1998) is the longest reign in this entire list bar none. Ledley King's seven years and Hugo Lloris's eight years are the only other Spurs captaincies to pass the five-year mark — everyone else has served three seasons or fewer.

Who has captained the longest?

John Terry's 13 years at Chelsea is the longest single captaincy across all six clubs, comfortably ahead of Steven Gerrard's 12 years at Liverpool and Gary Mabbutt's 11 years at Tottenham. At the other end, Granit Xhaka's brief 2019 spell at Arsenal — stripped of the armband within weeks after clashing with fans — is the shortest reign of any captain on these six lists.

Who is captain right now?

Martin Ødegaard leads Arsenal, Reece James leads Chelsea, Virgil van Dijk leads Liverpool, Bernardo Silva leads Manchester City, Bruno Fernandes leads Manchester United, and Cristian Romero leads Tottenham.

Methodology & sources

Only primary, permanent club captains are listed — vice-captains, caretaker armband-wearers standing in for an injured or absent captain, and single-match stand-ins are excluded unless they were subsequently confirmed as the club's own permanent captain. Where a captaincy change happened mid-season, the transition year is credited to both the outgoing and incoming captain. Figures are cross-checked against each club's official history pages and independent football history sources before publication.
