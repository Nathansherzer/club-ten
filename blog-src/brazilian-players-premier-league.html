Brazilian Players in the Premier League: The Greatest at Every Big Six Club

17 August 2026 · 5 min read · Data through end of 2025/26 season

No country has sent more technically gifted players to the Premier League than Brazil. From Gilberto Silva's invincible season at Arsenal to Ederson redefining the goalkeeper position at Manchester City, Brazilians have shaped the modern Premier League more than any other nation outside the British Isles. Here is how they rank at each of the Big Six.

Arsenal

Arsenal's Brazilian connection runs deep. Gilberto Silva anchored the Invincibles midfield in 2003/04, while Gabriel Magalhães and Gabriel Martinelli are now among the most important players at the club — with both already in the top two for all-time Brazilian appearances at Arsenal despite still being in their mid-twenties.

Chelsea

Chelsea have had more Brazilian Premier League appearances than any other Big Six club. Willian leads with 234 — more than any Brazilian at any rival — ahead of David Luiz and Ramires. Thiago Silva's 113 appearances at the age he joined the club make him one of the most remarkable Brazilian signings in Premier League history. Estêvão, at just 18, is already building a legacy.

Liverpool

Liverpool's Brazilian contingent has been relatively small but packed with quality. Roberto Firmino's 232 Premier League appearances made him the cornerstone of Klopp's title-winning side. Alisson Becker has established himself as one of the greatest goalkeepers in the club's history. Fabinho and Philippe Coutinho round out a group of Brazilians who all played significant roles at Anfield.

Manchester City

Manchester City's Brazilian spine has been the foundation of their Premier League dominance. Ederson leads with 277 appearances and has redefined what a goalkeeper can offer — his passing ability as much a weapon as his shot-stopping. Fernandinho, just one appearance behind at 264, spent a decade as City's engine room. Together they form arguably the greatest Brazilian duo in Premier League history.

Manchester United

United's Brazilian history is headlined by Fred, Casemiro, Rafael and Anderson — four very different players who between them covered 504 Premier League appearances in red. Casemiro's impact on United's midfield was immediate and transformative. Matheus Cunha, arriving from Wolves in 2025, is the latest Brazilian to make the move to Old Trafford.

Tottenham

Spurs have had fewer Brazilians than most of their rivals, but Lucas Moura's dramatic semi-final hat-trick against Ajax in 2019 gave them one of the most memorable Brazilian moments in Premier League club football. Richarlison and Sandro have also left their mark at the club.
