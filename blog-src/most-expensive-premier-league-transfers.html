The Most Expensive Premier League-to-Premier League Transfers of All Time

24 August 2026 · 5 min read · Fees in euros

The Premier League's spending power has turned domestic transfers into nine-figure deals. When clubs buy from each other, no foreign exchange rate applies — just pure market price. Here are the biggest PL-to-PL transfers ever recorded, club by club.

Liverpool's record signing leads the all-time list, followed by City's blockbuster Grealish deal and Arsenal's capture of Declan Rice — all within a few hundred million euros of each other.

Manchester United

Harry Maguire's £80m move from Leicester in 2019 made him the most expensive defender in history at the time. It also began an era of record-breaking purchases at United that delivered little on the pitch — a pattern that shaped the club's subsequent decade.
