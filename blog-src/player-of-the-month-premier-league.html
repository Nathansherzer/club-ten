Premier League Player of the Month: Full History and Winners

By Club Ten · Published 1 September 2026 · Data through 2025–26

Jürgen Klinsmann won the very first Premier League Player of the Month award, for his performances in August 1994 while at Tottenham Hotspur. Since then it's been awarded well over 350 times, with Sergio Agüero, Harry Kane and Mohamed Salah tied for the most career wins at 7 apiece.

This list overlaps with Club Ten's 1 September daily puzzles across all six clubs. Spoiler warning if you haven't played those yet.

A quick history of the award

The award launched alongside the Premier League's third season and has changed sponsor names four times: the Carling Premiership Player of the Month (1994–2001), the Barclaycard Premiership Player of the Month (2001–2004), the Barclays Player of the Month (2004–2016), and today's EA Sports Player of the Month.

Klinsmann's August 1994 award was also something of a statement. He'd joined Tottenham that summer under a cloud of scepticism from English pundits who doubted a player with his reputation for diving would adapt to the Premier League, then scored on his debut against Sheffield Wednesday and kept scoring through August.

Fun facts and records

Manchester United have produced more Player of the Month winners than any other club in the competition's history.

Robbie Keane is the only player to have won the award while at three different clubs. Twelve other players have won it for two different clubs each.

Only two players have won it three times in a single season: Mohamed Salah and Marcus Rashford. Nine players have won in back-to-back consecutive months, including Dennis Bergkamp, Cristiano Ronaldo and Harry Kane.

Why Klinsmann's win mattered

It's easy to forget now, but Klinsmann's 1994 move to Tottenham was treated as a gamble by a lot of the English press, built almost entirely around his reputation from that summer's World Cup for going down easily. Winning the very first Player of the Month award, in his first month in the country, flipped that narrative before the season had even settled, and he finished that debut campaign with 20 goals and the Football Writers' Player of the Year award alongside it.

Methodology & sources

Award history, sponsorship naming, and all-time records are cross-checked against Wikipedia's Premier League Player of the Month article and Premier League's own official award history. Per-club winner lists show each club's 10 most recent different winners as of the end of the 2025–26 season; a player who has won multiple times for the same club appears once, at their most recent win.
