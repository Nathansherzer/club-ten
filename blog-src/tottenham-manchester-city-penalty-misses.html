What's a Penalty? Spurs Fans Have a Point

By Club Ten · Published 10 November 2026

When Club Ten asked fans to name their club's ten most recent different Premier League penalty missers, one Tottenham supporter on Twitter wondered what a penalty even was.

The joke gets better when you compare Spurs with Manchester City.

A twelve-year gap

To complete Tottenham's list, you have to go back to Darren Anderton against Newcastle in January 2001. City's oldest answer is Edin Džeko at Arsenal in January 2013.

Same question. A twelve-year difference in how far back you need to remember.

So do City simply get more penalties?

In the ten years to November 2026, StatMuse records 65 penalties won by City, the most in the Premier League. Tottenham won 35. Almost twice as many opportunities for City to add another unfortunate name to the list.

But getting the penalty is only half the story. In that same window, City missed 18. Spurs missed five.

Then there's the taker

Harry Kane scored 33 Premier League penalties for Tottenham and missed four across his Spurs career. Repeated goals by the same reliable player don't add new answers to a puzzle about different penalty missers.

City's list, meanwhile, includes Agüero, De Bruyne, Gündoğan, Mahrez, Sterling, Jesus and Haaland. An extraordinary collection of footballers. Also an extraordinary collection of people who have given City fans reason to watch a penalty through their fingers.

More than a memory test

That's what made this question more than a memory test. Two clubs, two very different penalty histories, hidden inside the same ten-answer challenge.

Anderton's name was recorded just eight times in our tracking of the Spurs puzzle. Hardly surprising: remembering Kane is one thing. Remembering a Steve Harper penalty save from 2001 is another level of commitment.

Perhaps the next question isn't "what's a penalty?" It's which misses you've managed to forget. Think you can do better? Play today's Spurs puzzle or today's Man City puzzle and find out.

Methodology & sources

Penalty totals are StatMuse's rolling ten-year figures to November 2026; Kane's figures cover his Spurs Premier League career. The eight Anderton answers are recorded correct-guess events from 30 October to 9 November 2026 on the Club Ten puzzle, not eight verified individuals or a complete count of everyone who played.

Sources: City penalties won (StatMuse), Spurs penalties won (StatMuse), City misses (StatMuse), Spurs misses (StatMuse), Kane's penalty record (StatMuse), the Anderton miss (BBC Sport match report), and the Džeko miss (The Guardian match report).
