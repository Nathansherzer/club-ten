The All-Time European Goals Records at Arsenal, Chelsea, Liverpool, Man City, Man United and Spurs

10 August 2026 · 5 min read · Data through end of 2025/26 season

Which player has scored the most European goals for your club? We looked at every goal scored in UEFA competition — Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, UEFA Cup, and Cup Winners' Cup — for each of the Premier League's Big Six. The results span six decades of European football.

Arsenal

Thierry Henry's 42 European goals are untouchable at Arsenal, scored across five Champions League campaigns. Theo Walcott and Robin van Persie sit second and third, with Bukayo Saka already fourth despite Arsenal's relatively recent return to European football.

#PlayerGoals
1Thierry Henry42
2Theo Walcott21
3Robin van Persie20
4Bukayo Saka18
5Aaron Ramsey17

Chelsea

Didier Drogba is the undisputed king of European football at Chelsea with 36 goals — including that iconic Champions League final equaliser in 2012. Frank Lampard sits second, a reminder of how complete a player he was. Olivier Giroud's 18 goals are particularly impressive given his role as a squad player during Chelsea's 2021 Champions League win.

#PlayerGoals
1Didier Drogba36
2Frank Lampard25
3Olivier Giroud18
4Fernando Torres17
5Peter Osgood16

Liverpool

Mohamed Salah's 53 European goals make him not only Liverpool's all-time leader but one of the greatest European scorers in English football history. Steven Gerrard in second with 41 shows how deep Liverpool's European DNA runs — Gerrard scored in three separate European finals. Roger Hunt's 17 goals are a reminder that Liverpool's continental pedigree dates back to the 1960s.

#PlayerGoals
1Mohamed Salah53
2Steven Gerrard41
3Sadio Mané26
4Roberto Firmino24
5Michael Owen22

Manchester City

Sergio Agüero's 43 European goals at City may never be surpassed — but Erling Haaland is already at 37 and showing no signs of slowing. The gap between Haaland and Phil Foden in third (24) underlines just how extraordinary both strikers have been. Neil Young's 11 goals from the early 1970s are a nod to City's brief but memorable first spell in European football.

#PlayerGoals
1Sergio Agüero43
2Erling Haaland37
3Phil Foden24
4Raheem Sterling24
5Gabriel Jesus21

Manchester United

Wayne Rooney leads with 39 European goals, just one ahead of Ruud van Nistelrooy — whose 38 goals came in a remarkable eight-season spell including a legendary 2002/03 Champions League campaign. Ryan Giggs (29) and Denis Law (28) show the depth of United's European history, stretching from the Busby Babes era to the treble and beyond.

#PlayerGoals
1Wayne Rooney39
2Ruud van Nistelrooy38
3Ryan Giggs29
4Denis Law28
5Marcus Rashford26

Tottenham Hotspur

Harry Kane's 45 European goals are the record at Spurs by a distance — and would rank him highly at most other clubs too. Son Heung-min in second with 27 underlines how much of Spurs' modern European output came from that partnership. Martin Chivers' 22 goals from the early 1970s UEFA Cup runs are a reminder of Spurs' pre-Premier League European pedigree.

#PlayerGoals
1Harry Kane45
2Son Heung-min27
3Jermain Defoe23
4Martin Chivers22
5Mark Falco13

The big picture

Mohamed Salah (53) leads all Big Six players, ahead of Harry Kane (45), Sergio Agüero (43), Thierry Henry (42) and Steven Gerrard (41). Liverpool and Manchester City — the two clubs that dominated the last decade of Champions League football in England — own the top two spots.
