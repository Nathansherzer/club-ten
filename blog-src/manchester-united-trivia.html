Manchester United Trivia: You Can't Win Anything with Kids

By Club Ten · Published 6 September 2026

On the opening day of the 1995-96 season, the BBC pundit Alan Hansen watched a Manchester United side featuring several academy graduates lose 3-1 at Aston Villa and delivered the line he has never been allowed to forget: "You can't win anything with kids." Nine months later, United's academy generation had helped the club win the Premier League and FA Cup Double. It is probably the most replayed wrong prediction in English football, and the story of how it aged so badly is better than the quote itself.

United sold their own title winners on purpose

The context people forget is that Hansen wasn't reacting to nothing. That summer Ferguson dismantled an experienced core, selling Paul Ince to Inter Milan, Mark Hughes to Chelsea and Andrei Kanchelskis to Everton. He made no senior signing to replace them, choosing instead to trust United's academy graduates. So when United lost 3-1 at Villa Park, an experienced pundit looking at that team sheet and drawing the obvious conclusion wasn't being unreasonable. David Beckham came off the bench and pulled one back in the 84th minute, a small hint of what was coming.

The Class of '92 were already winners

What Hansen couldn't see from the outside was that these weren't just any young players. Beckham, Giggs, Butt and Gary Neville had already won the 1992 FA Youth Cup under the legendary youth coach Eric Harrison, beating Crystal Palace over two legs. Paul Scholes was a runner-up with the side that reached the following season's final, and Phil Neville would captain United to the trophy in 1995. Collectively they became known as the Class of '92, and they had been winning together long before the senior team ever leaned on them.

The Double, and the cream suits

United went on to win the 1995-96 league title, pipping Kevin Keegan's Newcastle after overhauling a large gap in the second half of the season. Then came the FA Cup final against Liverpool at Wembley, a match now remembered almost as much for Liverpool arriving in matching cream Armani suits as for the football. With five minutes left, David James punched David Beckham's corner clear. It dropped to Eric Cantona on the edge of the box, and Cantona adjusted his body and volleyed it back through a crowd of players into the net. 1-0. United had the Double, becoming the first English club to win the League and FA Cup double twice.

The kicker

The kids didn't stop there. The Class of '92 became the spine of the most successful era in the club's history, culminating in the 1999 Treble, the Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League in a single season. Between them, that core group would accumulate dozens of major honours across their careers. Hansen, to his credit, took the joke well for the next two decades, but the line follows him around to this day precisely because United answered it so completely and so quickly.
