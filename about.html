What is Club Ten?

Club Ten is a free daily football trivia game. Each day you get a top-10 challenge about your chosen club: name the last ten managers, the all-time top scorers, the most-capped players, and more.

How it works

Pick your club once and the game remembers it. Every day at midnight London time a new puzzle drops. You have three lives, and a wrong guess costs one. Your streak, games played, and perfect scores are all saved in your browser; no account needed.

Who makes Club Ten

Club Ten is an independent project, built and run by one person, a lifelong football fan who wanted a quick, honest daily quiz that respects your time: no sign-up, no account, just play. It isn't affiliated with the Premier League or any club. Questions, corrections and partnership enquiries all go to the contact page.

How we research and verify the puzzles

Every puzzle is researched before it goes live and date-stamped, so you can always see how current the data is. Answers are cross-checked against more than one source (Wikipedia's season-by-season appearance and goal tables, the Premier League's official player statistics, each club's own historical records, and stats providers such as StatMuse and Opta) rather than trusting a single page. Where a ranking rests on a judgement call, such as how ties are broken, which competitions count, or the cut-off date, those rules are spelled out in the small print under each puzzle, so the answer is checkable rather than just asserted.

Corrections

We work hard to get every answer right, but football data is messy and mistakes do happen. If you spot one, tell us through the contact page and we'll check it and put it right. Getting it accurate matters more to us than having been right first time.

A note on images

Club Ten is text-only, with no club crests, kits, or player photos. This keeps the site free of football image licensing issues and loads fast on mobile.
